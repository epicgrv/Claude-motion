**Hook mechanics (same as the reference):** the first sentence is an absurd image (a check for a jet). Line 5 is the buddy voicing what the viewer is thinking. Line 9 names the
concept. Twist #1 is the company laughing it off, then the court. Twist #2 is that the "joke" was real enough that they changed the price. The video ends hard on a two-word punchline, and the
first frame (the check) works as a loop.

**Alternative hooks (pick one if you prefer):**
